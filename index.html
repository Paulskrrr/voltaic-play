How to play

Hold the boulevard. Where you build matters as much as what you build: terraces lend range, tenements block sight, big towers need room to fit, and towers that touch each other help or hinder one another.

  • Drag a tower card onto the map, or tap a card and then the map, to build.
  • R rotates a footprint. Shift-click keeps building. Click a robot to inspect it.
  • Space sends the next wave. Early waves pay bonus gold.
  • Click a tower to upgrade it. At level 3 it picks one of two paths.
  • On maps with gold-bordered lots you can only build on the lots and terraces.
  • P pauses, M mutes, Esc cancels.
